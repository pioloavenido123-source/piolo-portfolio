Web-Based POS System

Role: Project Manager — BS Information Technology

Spearheaded the development of a web-based point-of-sale system accessible through any device's browser. The system smoothens administrative operations, transactions, inventory management, rewards and redemptions, and handles multi-branch management — allowing owners and admins to monitor revenue, billing of materials (inventory items), account status, and shifting schedules across all branches from a single interface.

As Project Manager, I led the team through the full software development lifecycle — from requirements gathering and planning to implementation, testing, and final defense. I managed task delegation, timelines, and coordination between team members to ensure deliverables met both academic and technical standards.
